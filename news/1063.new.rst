Add hooks for ``openstacksdk`` (imported as ``openstack``), ``keystoneauth1`` and
``os_service_types``. These collect the distribution metadata that ``pbr`` reads
at import time, the data files that ``openstack.config`` (including its vendor
profiles) and ``os_service_types`` load at run-time, and the service proxies and
stevedore auth plugins that are resolved by name and are therefore invisible to
static analysis.
